# Countryside models

These models use the CC0 1.0 license.

- Quaternius created the [animated animals](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), [crops](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatecrops.html), [modular ruins](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularruins.html) and [modular dungeon](https://quaternius.com/packs/modulardungeon.html) props. GLB conversions are from [Quaternius Showcase](https://github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase).
- Kay Lousberg created the green market, windmill and watermill from the [KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Medieval-Hexagon-Pack-1.0). The atlas was sampled into vertex colours, keeping the original rotor pivots.
- Kenney created the rowboat and sailboat from the [Watercraft Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/watercraft-kit). The compressed meshes are from [World of Claudecraft](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft), with timber and canvas remapped to the InkBloom palette.
